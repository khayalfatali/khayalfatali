## Hero "5 pillars" (homepage)

> "See your whole business click into place"

1. **Take payments** — Sell anything in person and online with a point of sale platform that works for whatever you sell.
2. **Manage your team** — Run payroll, schedule shifts, and take care of admin so your team can focus on performing their best.
3. **Grow your customer base** — Set up the right marketing campaigns and loyalty program to welcome new customers and keep them coming back for more.
4. **Control your cash flow** — Bring your revenue under one roof. Instantly access your funds, save for future expenses, and get quicker access to loans.
5. **Connect your favorite apps** — Square partners with hundreds of apps so you can manage your entire business without jumping between.

## Yeri parity mapping (what we adopt vs. adapt)

- **Adopt**: 5-pillar hero framing, Business-types mega category with sub-items, processing-fees block, "Sign in" separate from "Sign up".
- **Adapt**: Yeri is Azerbaijan-first and mobile-only (Tap to Pay on iPhone), so Hardware category collapses to zero — hardware pillar is a *differentiator*, not a product line.
- **Adopt**: Product sub-groupings (Payments / Staff / Customers / Banking / Developers) — lighter versions for launch scope.
- **Adapt**: Pricing tiers stay as Starter / Business / Scale (Yeri plans) but add a processing-fees block like Square underneath.
